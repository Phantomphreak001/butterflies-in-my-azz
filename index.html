Expectations

Guild Rules

Short, respectful, and easy to follow.

  1. Be decent. No harassment, bigotry, or drama dumps in guild chat. Disagree like adults.
  2. Show up ready. On raid nights: consumables, repaired gear, addons set, and voice ready 10 minutes early.
  3. Communicate. Can't make a raid? Say so early. Life happens — ghosts don't help the pull timer.
  4. Loot fairness. Follow the loot rules announced for that night (MS > OS, need/greed, or council). No ninja loot.
  5. Help the guild. Share mats tips, help alts, and keep the vibe fun. Butterflies stick together.
  6. Officers decide. If something's unclear, ask an officer. Their call stands until it's changed.
